// About

Where models meet the systems that run them.

Most of my work sits at the seam between machine learning and the infrastructure that keeps it alive in production — deploying open-weight LLMs like gpt-oss and Gemma behind Nginx, building retrieval pipelines over ElasticSearch and vector stores, and wiring the CI/CD and container orchestration that makes all of it reliable.

I like problems with two halves: a modeling half (RAG, embeddings, fine-tuning, classification) and an engineering half (latency, uptime, scale). Most of my recent projects — matching candidates to job descriptions with gpt-oss RAG, semantic search over bulk vector data, image-explanation with Gemma — needed both.

I studied Computer Science & Engineering with a Machine Learning major at Lovely Professional University, and I've been building production ML/DevOps systems professionally since.
